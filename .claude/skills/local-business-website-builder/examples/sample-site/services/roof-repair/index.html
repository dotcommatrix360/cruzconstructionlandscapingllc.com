Roof repair in Boise

Most leaks come from flashing, worn pipe boots or wind-lifted shingles. We find the source, photograph it, and fix it — usually in one visit.

What we repair

  • Active leaks and water stains
  • Missing or wind-damaged shingles
  • Flashing around chimneys, skylights and walls
  • Hail and storm damage (see signs of hail damage)

How it works

Call or request an inspection online. You'll get photos and a written estimate before any work begins.
