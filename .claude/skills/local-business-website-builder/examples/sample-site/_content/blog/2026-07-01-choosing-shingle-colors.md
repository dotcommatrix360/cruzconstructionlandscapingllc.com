Your roof is up to 40% of what people see from the street, so color matters.

## Start with fixed colors

Brick, stone and trim colors won't change — pick shingles that complement them.

| Siding | Shingle colors that usually work |
|---|---|
| White or light gray | Charcoal, black, weathered wood |
| Beige or tan | Brown, driftwood, slate |
| Red brick | Charcoal, black, brown |

## Think about heat

Lighter shingles reflect more sun, which can help in hot Boise summers.
