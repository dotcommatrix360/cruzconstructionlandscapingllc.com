A new roof is one of the largest investments in a home, so the details under the shingles matter as much as the color on top.

## What a replacement includes

Every replacement starts with a full tear-off down to the decking. Damaged or soft boards are replaced before anything new goes on, then we install underlayment, ice and water shield along the eaves, new drip edge and flashing, and the shingles themselves.

## Ventilation and attic health

Many older Treasure Valley homes have too little attic ventilation, which shortens shingle life and raises cooling costs. We check intake and exhaust and recommend changes only when they are needed.

## Choosing materials

We walk you through shingle lines and colors with real samples on your driveway. Our guide to [choosing shingle colors](/choosing-shingle-colors/) is a good place to start.

## Cleanup

The crew runs a magnetic sweep for nails across the yard and driveway every day. [Contact us](/contact/) to schedule an estimate, or see our [repair service](/services/roof-repair/) if your roof may only need a fix.
