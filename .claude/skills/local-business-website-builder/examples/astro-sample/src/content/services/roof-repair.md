Most roof problems start small: a lifted shingle, a cracked vent boot, flashing that pulled away from a chimney. Repaired early, they rarely need more than a few hours of work. Left alone through a Boise winter, the same problem can rot decking and stain ceilings.

## How a repair visit works

We start with a free inspection. The crew checks the shingles, flashing, vents, gutters and attic, and sends you a photo report that shows exactly what we found — so you can see the damage instead of taking our word for it.

## Common repairs

- Replacing missing or wind-damaged shingles and matching the color as closely as possible
- Resealing or replacing flashing around chimneys, skylights and walls
- Replacing cracked plumbing vent boots, a frequent source of slow attic leaks
- Hail and storm damage repairs, with documentation for your insurer

## When repair isn't enough

If the inspection shows widespread granule loss or soft decking, we'll tell you plainly and explain when a [roof replacement](/services/roof-replacement/) makes more sense. Read [how to spot hail damage](/how-to-spot-hail-damage/) or [request an inspection](/contact/).
