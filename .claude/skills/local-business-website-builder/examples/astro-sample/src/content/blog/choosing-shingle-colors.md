Your roof is up to 40% of what people see from the street, so color matters more than most homeowners expect when they plan a replacement.

## Start with fixed colors

Brick, stone and trim colors won't change — pick shingles that complement them rather than compete with them.

| Siding | Shingle colors that usually work |
|---|---|
| White or light gray | Charcoal, black, weathered wood |
| Beige or tan | Brown, driftwood, slate |
| Red brick | Charcoal, black, brown |

## Think about heat

Lighter shingles reflect more sun, which can help attic temperatures in hot Boise summers. Darker shingles hide streaks and granule loss better as the roof ages.

## Check the neighborhood

Some HOAs publish approved color lists. Ask for the list before you order samples.
