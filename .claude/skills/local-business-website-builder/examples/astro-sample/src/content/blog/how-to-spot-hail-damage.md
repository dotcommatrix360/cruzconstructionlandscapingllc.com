After a hailstorm, most roof damage isn't visible from the street — but a few signs are. **Stay off the roof**; everything below can be checked from the ground or a window.

## 1. Dents on gutters and downspouts

Soft metal shows impacts first. Round dents on gutters, downspouts or the AC unit mean the roof took hits too.

## 2. Granules at the downspout outlet

A pile of dark, sand-like granules after a storm means shingles lost their protective layer.

## 3. Cracked or missing shingles

Use binoculars and look for dark circular spots where granules were knocked off, cracked or split shingle edges, and shingles missing entirely.

## 4. Damaged vents and flashing

Metal roof vents and flashing dent easily. Damage here often means leaks later.

## 5. Interior water stains

Check ceilings and the attic for new stains in the days after a storm.

> If you see two or more of these signs, schedule an inspection before filing an insurance claim.
