A leak rarely waits for a convenient time. These steps limit the damage until a repair crew gets there.

## Contain the water

Move furniture and electronics, put down a bucket, and lay towels to stop the water from spreading across floors.

## Relieve ceiling bulges

If the ceiling is bulging with water, a small hole at the lowest point lets it drain into a bucket instead of collapsing the drywall.

## Document everything

Take photos of the stain, the source if visible, and damaged items — your insurer will ask for them.

## Don't climb on a wet roof
